Recommendations

Please note again these are all general recommendations based off of our analysis of the dataset which showed the relationships between hypertension, heart disease, average glucose levels, BMI index, and smking status of someone to their stroke status (If they ever suffered from a stroke or not)


Hypertension and Heart Disease

As suggested from the graphs in the previous page a large percentage of those who suffered from a stroke also suffered from hypertension and/or heart disease.

If you suffer from hypertension and heart disease, you are generally at a high chance of suffering from a stroke is quite high, so it is best for you to not to perform any extensive exercise.

People who suffer from either hypertension (high blood pressure) or heart disease are at a much more higher risk to suffer from a stroke.
If you suffer from both of these conditions, you must be even more vary of your health since that highers your chance of suffering from a stroke even more.


Average Blood Glucose Levels and BMI Index

Average Glucose Levels and BMI Indexes are linked when it comes to the weight of a person. In general, those who tend to have a higher average glucose level, also suffer from a high BMI Index.
From the graph in the previous page those who tend to have a higher Average Glucose Level and BMI index tend to be the ones who have suffered from a stroke, thus it is advised to keep an eye on those numbers.
If you suffer from hypertension and/or heart disease you must be incredibly careful of your average glucose levels and BMI index because it would affect you at a much bigger scale compared to individuals without
hypertension or heart disease.
Both high or low average glucose level and/or BMI index can strain and fatigue the heart which increases your chances of suffering from a stroke.


Average Blood Glucose Levels

It is best for you to keep your average glucose levels between 70 and 100 for a healthy amount.
If your average glucose level goes above 100 you must try to bring it back down by reducing your sugar and carbohydrate intake or insulin injections however that must be done under the care of a professional.
If your average glucose level goes below 70 you must try to bring it up by increasing your sugar and carbohydrate intake.


BMI Index

It is best for you to keep your BMI index between 18 and 29 for a healthy body.
If your BMI index is above 29 you must try to lose weight to bring it down to a healthy amount.
If your BMI index is below 18 you must try to gain more weight to bring it back up.
Both losing weight and gaining weight must be done moderately and in a healthy manner or it will just strain and stress the heart even further.


Age

It is expected that as the age goes up the chances of suffering from a stroke increases as suggested by the graph in the previous page.
So, we must all be careful as we age and reduce our risks of suffering froma stroke since the increase in age is already playing a major role in getting a stroke.
